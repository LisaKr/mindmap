**TOKYO**

**Day trips from Tokyo:**

- [Shosenkyo](https://maps.app.goo.gl/oafgH7CPX1PbmhZQ7) – beautiful gorge hike \+ rock formations, if lucky could see Fuji, great for foliage. 2h20 min by car from shinjuku station, unsure about public transportation
- [Fujikawaguchiko](https://maps.app.goo.gl/XB7gT1ACtBKi1BUr8) very popular spot to see Fuji, super crowded, 90 min by car or 1h50min by public transport from shinjuku station
- [A ryokan](https://maps.app.goo.gl/Tf9Rts169vh9rEWT7) where you have a wonderful view on Fuji from your room (prolly expensive), 1h40min by car and 2h40min by public transport  from shinjuku station
- [Komagatake Ropeway Top Station](https://maps.app.goo.gl/RswsAGtAL1DvJKn76), A cable car to a panoramic spot to see lake Ashi + Fuji, but no good when it is clouded, 1h46min by car, 3h30min by public transport   from shinjuku station
- Mount Mitake area:  (1h52min by car, 3h6min by public transport) from shinjuku station
  - [A charming ryokan \+ onsen](https://maps.app.goo.gl/u7oVj97c7SGuyfVS7) on mount Mitake (could hike \+ stay)  
  - [Mt Mitake itself](https://maps.app.goo.gl/BX7n6xFuHbeVW85M9), beautiful  
  - [The cable car](https://maps.app.goo.gl/GUjntd49FpVYszFw9) for Mt Mitake  
  - [A sake and tasting bar](https://maps.app.goo.gl/o86EaUpPxahqWDdJ9) along the river around there  
- [Kawagoe](https://maps.app.goo.gl/5k6r6pBeWRfoDtZbA), Edo-period castle town, unsure why I added it, cute old town ig, 1h18min by car, 56min by train from shinjuku station
